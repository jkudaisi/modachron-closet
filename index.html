01 / How it learns

Swipe. It listens.

Swipe right on outfits you love and left on the ones you don't. Every swipe is read against the pieces in that look, so ModaChron builds your closet identity: the colors you reach for, baggy or fitted, casual or formal.

The first few suggestions might miss. Over time it learns, until the first outfit of the day is the one you wear.

05 / ModaShop Coming soon

Shop pieces picked for your closet, and sell the ones you don't wear.

ModaShop is ModaChron's built-in marketplace to buy and resell clothes. It shows you pieces that match the wardrobe you already own, not an endless feed.

  • Picks, not browsingListings suggested because they fit your closet and style, on Home and as closet recommendations.
  • Sell what you don't wearList straight from your closet. A Sell Review swipe deck helps you decide what to let go, and listed items get a Listed badge.
  • OffersBuyers make offers, and sellers accept, decline or counter. Get notified when there's an offer, and when it's sold, shipped and delivered.
  • Easy to startList before you set up payouts. When you see "You sold it!", set up how you get paid.

ModaShop learns your taste from your closet and your Home swipes, then suggests pieces that fill gaps and go with what you own. Your closet is where selling starts.

08 / FAQ

Quick answers.

What does ModaChron do?

ModaChron recommends outfits from your existing wardrobe using smart styling logic, weather data and your schedule, like a personal stylist in your pocket.

How does ModaChron use weather and my calendar?

The app checks local forecasts and upcoming events so outfit suggestions match temperature, rain and occasion, whether you are heading to work, traveling or staying in.

Is ModaChron available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download ModaChron on the App Store or Google Play to start building outfits from your closet.

Do I need a large wardrobe to get started?

No. Many users start with a modest closet. ModaChron helps you remix core pieces in new ways and spot versatile gaps only when you are ready to shop.

Where can I get help or read your policies?

Visit app support for help, or read our privacy policy and terms of service.
